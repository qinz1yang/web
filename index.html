Current Focus

I work at the intersection of formal mathematics, differential geometry, and geometric analysis.

Differential Geometry in Lean 4

I develop and maintain a formal proof library for differential geometry and geometric analysis in Lean 4. The project now includes a complete formalization of Hamilton's 1982 three-manifold theorem.

Selected formalized results include:

  • Hamilton's three-manifold theorem
  • Short-time existence for Ricci flow
  • Hamilton–Ivey pinching estimate
  • Bochner and Weitzenböck identities
  • Levi-Civita connection and integration by parts
